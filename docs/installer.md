Production-Ready Standardization & Installer Specifications
---------------------------------------------------------------

To ensure rapid, error-free deployment across Seattle business corridors, the visual and physical specifications have been locked down to strict industrial standards. Reliability and ease of installation take precedence over aesthetics.

* * * * *

1. Pre-Applied Natural Adhesive & Clear Backing
--------------------------------------------------

-   Factory Pre-Cut Configuration: Every individual block comes with the 100% natural rubber-resin tape pre-cut to the exact footprint of the corner chassis edge, ensuring zero edge-fray or exposed adhesive lines.
-   High-Visibility Clear Liner: The installer-facing side features an extended, heavy-duty clear backing pull-tab.
-   Deployment Workflow: The installer snaps the block off the main strip, slides the rubber insulating cap over the recessed backbone stub, peels away the clear tab, and presses the node firmly into the ceiling corner. The clear liner allows the technician to visually confirm the tape matrix is fully intact before mounting.

* * * * *

2. Standardized Color Protocols
----------------------------------

The custom styling has been replaced with universally recognized safety colorways to guarantee immediate identification by facilities engineers, safety inspectors, and server technicians:

-   Enclosure Color: The entire outer shell is molded in Standard Fire Red (matching international fire protection infrastructure standards), allowing emergency personnel to instantly locate network nodes.
-   Interface Port: The USB-B receptacle utilizes the standard, industry-default housing color. This maintains strict part uniformity, allowing standard off-the-shelf thumb-stick cameras, microphones, and laser sensors to seat seamlessly with maximum mechanical connection stability.

* * * * *

3. Physical Node Production Profile
---------------------------------------

| Component | Engineering Standard Compliance |
| Enclosure Outer Body | Standard Fire Red UV-stabilized polymer (NFPA-compliant visual profile). |
