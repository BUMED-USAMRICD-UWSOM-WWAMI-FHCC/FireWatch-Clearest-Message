Updated Recessed Snap-Channel & Thermal Mounting Specification
-----------------------------------------------------------------

To ensure a completely flush mount after segmenting a block, the hardware design has been updated to recess the strip's backbone pathway into the core body of the corner module.

1. Recessed Internal Snap-Channel Geometry
----------------------------------------------

-   Zero-Protrusion Clearance: The master backbone strip runs through a deep, internal center-channel molded into the back of the block.
-   Flush Snap Clearance: When a module is snapped off, the fracture point occurs deep within this internal channel, leaving a sub-surface stub.
-   Recessed Rubber Capping: The protective rubber insulating caps slide over the open gold lattice stub entirely within the channel boundary. Because the cap does not extend past the outer walls of the module housing, it will never rub against the ceiling or wall surfaces or force the block away from its mounting plane.

2. Dual-Function Thermal Adhesive Interface
-----------------------------------------------

-   Material Composition: The mounting pads use a high-bond, fiberglass-reinforced thermal interface material (TIM) tape instead of standard foam adhesives.
-   Simultaneous Tasks:

    1.  Mechanical Bonding: Secures the lightweight 1.2 cubic inch block to textured drywall, steel beams, or aluminum framing without mechanical fasteners.
    2.  Thermal Dissipation Bridge: Acts as a continuous conduction path. It draws operational heat away from the internal 2oz/3oz copper power leads and channels it directly into the structural ceiling or wall matrix, using the building itself as an expansive heat sink.

-   Fire Safety Margin: This constant thermal bleed prevents any localized heat accumulation, keeping the internal environment within nominal tolerances even when delivering maximum power to connected USB peripherals.
